Traditions

Moments of a Telugu Wedding

Swipe through the rituals you'll witness

1

Mangala Snanam

The bride and groom are bathed in turmeric and oil and blessed by elders as the bride and groom to be.

2

Kasi Yatra

The groom playfully sets off for Kasi to take up an ascetic life, until the bride's brother persuades him to marry instead.

3

Kanyadanam

The bride's parents place her hand in the groom's, entrusting him with their most precious gift.

4at 4:46 AM

Jeelakarra Bellam

At the exact muhurtham, a paste of cumin and jaggery is placed on each other's heads, an inseparable bond through life's bitter and sweet.

5

Mangalya Dharana

The groom ties the sacred mangalsutram around the bride's neck with three knots.

6

Talambralu

The couple shower each other with turmeric rice and pearls, the most joyful and playful moment of the day.

7

Saptapadi

Seven steps together around the sacred fire, with seven vows for a lifetime.

8

Arundhati Darshanam

The couple are shown the Arundhati star, a symbol of devotion and an unbreakable union.
